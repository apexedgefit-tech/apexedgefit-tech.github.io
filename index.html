Why the usual approaches fail

The fitness industry thrives on quick fixes and aesthetic marketing. Almost everything it sells fails for one of three reasons.

  • They're built to end.

    30-day shreds, 90-day transformations and cut-first diets are designed for a fast result by a deadline. Nothing is built for the day it ends, so most people gain it all back.

  • They're built for everyone.

    Classes, apps, templates and influencer programs are written once and sold to everyone. None of them assesses how you move or what you can handle, so the same plan stalls or hurts the next person.

  • Nobody is watching.

    A gym membership gives you access, not a plan. Nobody tracks your progress or adjusts when your week changes, so the first hard month ends it.

My coaching starts with the questions they skip, adjusts every week and teaches you why it works, so the results are yours to keep.

Before you apply

Questions I get every week.

How much time does this take each week?

Your plan works around your time, not the other way around. This isn't a template you have to squeeze into. I design your program around your current life and build it in the right order to support your long-term goals.

What if I want to cancel?

Your first 30 days are covered by the Right Fit Guarantee. After that, coaching runs for the full term you choose.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. We work with the food you like and around the food you don't. You'll learn how to eat to fuel yourself and your goals, and when and why to track. You won't be forced into a restrictive diet or someone else's way of eating.

How do you track my progress?

Every week, through your check-ins. We track your lifts to make sure you keep progressing, your lifestyle metrics to make sure we're building the right habits, and your body composition, with progress photos and body measurements. We also track how you feel day to day: your energy, sleep and hunger, plus the sleep and recovery data from your wearable.

What if I have a busy week or I travel?

Your plan adjusts and flexes with your life. Every week I look at your feedback, your progress and what you've got going on, and we adjust from there. Whether it's a busy week or a trip, you keep making progress.

Do I need a gym?

No. I design your program around the equipment and space you have.
